Built for rowers

Personalized training. Clear feedback. Simple controls.

Personalized training zones

Enter your maximum and resting heart rates to calculate personalized training zones. Choose your target zone for each session.

Interval training

Select a workout from the built-in library or create your own, with work and recovery intervals tailored to your training.

Device connectivity

Pair RowPulser with a compatible Bluetooth heart-rate strap. When you're on the water, connect to a Bluetooth speaker for audible cues.

Practical features

Use battery-saving mode, hear session start and finish cues, and track how long you've spent in your target heart-rate zone.
